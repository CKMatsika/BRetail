# Payroll setup guide

Enable **Payroll** after enabling Human resources and Accounting. Payroll setup is company controlled and salary amounts are hidden unless the user has `payroll.compensation.view`.

## Rule versions

Create a source-linked rule version for the relevant currency and effective dates. The included 2026 Zimbabwe USD and ZWG templates are based on [ZIMRA's USD table](https://www.zimra.co.zw/?download=4523%3Ausd-paye-tax-tables-2026) and [ZWG table](https://www.zimra.co.zw/domestic-taxes/tax-tables?download=4522%3Azwg-paye-tax-tables-2026). A rule starts as a draft; a different user with `payroll.rules.approve` must review and approve it. Two approved rule sets for the same currency cannot cover overlapping effective dates.

Rule bands use `{ "up_to": number|null, "rate": percent, "deduct": amount }`. The final band must have `up_to: null`; thresholds must increase. The source name and URL are stored with the version. Rule approvals are audited.

Both templates leave NSSA parameters blank. NSSA currently publishes equal 4.5% employee/employer contributions against a USD 700 ceiling, and notes that the ceiling is gazetted quarterly. Enter those values with the NSSA source and period that apply to your payroll before approving a rule version; the PAYE table's validity dates do not establish the separate NSSA effective dates. POBS coverage includes employees older than 16 and younger than 65 who are Zimbabwean citizens or ordinarily resident in Zimbabwe. NSSA lists exemptions for nonresident foreign citizens, non-Zimbabwean diplomatic staff and domestic workers; casual work also has a monthly 18-day rule and a recurring-contract exception. HR stores the supporting citizenship, residence, category, identity and NSSA registration data under personal-data access controls. Payroll rejects a confirmed POBS flag when recorded information conflicts with those exemptions. For casual workers, payroll still requires an authorized monthly coverage review because it does not record actual days worked or contract recurrence.

The employer APWCS industry rate is configured separately on each approved rule version. Enter the employer's NSSA-assigned industry code, rate and source confirmation; BRetail does not assume every employer has the same rate. APWCS is an employer cost and is not deducted from employee net pay. The calculation uses basic salary, applies no earnings ceiling, and automatically excludes civil servants and employees age 16 or younger; employees older than 65 remain eligible. Mixed runs use the assigned industry rate from the approved USD rule and calculate each currency's premium against its native-currency basic salary. Confirm the current APWCS base and assigned rate with NSSA before approval. POBS and APWCS eligibility are distinct: POBS exclusions and casual-worker coverage review do not automatically remove APWCS.

## Salary terms and monthly runs

Authorized payroll users can create branch-scoped employee monthly salary terms with currency and effective dates. Terms record base cash salary, recurring taxable cash earnings, noncash taxable benefits, allowable PAYE deductions, monthly tax credits, other cash deductions and a checked NSSA eligibility flag. Overlapping terms for the same employee are rejected; starting a new term closes an open term the day before its effective date. Amounts are confidential and excluded from ordinary audit event details.

Create a monthly run by branch and currency. It uses the approved Zimbabwe rule set covering the entire month. Hires and departures can be included, and multiple effective-dated salary terms can be split across employed calendar days, as long as terms cover all employed dates without gaps/overlaps and remain in one currency. Salary, recurring earnings, benefits, deductions and credits are prorated per term by term days divided by calendar days in that month. The approved rule must cite the configured calendar-day earnings-proration policy; optional daily PAYE bands are not used by the current monthly averaging engine. One-off run adjustments are applied at their entered amounts. PAYE is calculated from cash earnings plus recorded taxable benefits, less allowable deductions, then applies credits (capped at tax before credits) and the AIDS levy. NSSA is calculated only for employees whose term is marked eligible and only when the approved rule contains applicable rates/ceiling in the same payroll currency. Enter salary amounts and credits from verified payroll source records.

A second user must approve the draft; approval locks the calculation. A payroll user can post an approved run only when the accounting period is open. Single-currency runs require payroll currency to equal the company base/ledger currency. Mixed USD + ZWG runs convert to the USD or ZWG company base currency using the saved pay-date FX snapshot. The posting credits net wages, PAYE/AIDS levy, NSSA and other deductions payable. Noncash taxable benefit amounts affect taxable income but are not added to gross cash wages; record their expense through the appropriate benefit or expense workflow. A posted journal can be reversed with a reason; a corrected run can then be created. After the bank transfer is actually made, an authorized payroll or accounting user can record one full net-wage settlement. The workflow checks recorded bank funds and the accounting period, then clears net wages payable; mixed settlement can use a matching base-currency account and foreign bank account. It does not initiate a transfer with the bank.

Users with both `payroll.runs.export` and `payroll.compensation.view` can download a register for an approved run or year-to-date totals for approved, non-reversed runs. Export events are audited, and spreadsheet formula-leading employee text is escaped. Users with compensation view permission can open an approved employee payslip and print or save it as a PDF from the browser. Payslips for reversed runs are unavailable.

## Email payslips

Email delivery is off by default. An administrator must set `PAYROLL_PAYSLIP_EMAIL_ENABLED=true` and configure a real mail transport and sender address; log, array and failover mailers are blocked. A payroll user with `payroll.payslips.email` and compensation-view permission can send the approved HTML pay statement to the employee email on file after confirming the action. Only non-reversed runs are eligible. The delivery audit stores an HMAC of the destination address, request idempotency data and the transport result; it does not store the raw email address or the payslip body. “Sent” means the configured transport accepted the message, not confirmation that it reached or was read by the employee. If an attempt remains in `sending`, verify provider logs before retrying because it may already have been accepted.

Mixed USD + ZWG remuneration can be recorded as two parallel effective-dated salary terms for the same employee (one term in each currency). Both terms must cover every day the employee is employed in the pay period; zero-valued terms can represent a currency that is not paid during one segment. Draft a **Mixed USD + ZWG** run and enter the actual payment date. The run requires an independently approved USD rule version for the full period and an approved ZWG conversion rate effective on the payment date. Hires, departures, and mid-month term changes require a sourced calendar-day earnings-proration policy on the approved USD rule. One-off mixed-run adjustments must name their currency. ZIMRA says to apply the USD PAYE tables and apportion tax across the currencies; BRetail follows the configured mixed-pay policy by allocating PAYE in proportion to each currency's share of converted taxable income. When NSSA coverage is confirmed, combined converted cash wages use the approved USD rule's verified USD ceiling and contributions are allocated by each currency's share of converted cash wages, following [NSSA's published USD ceiling](https://www.nssa.org.zw/Contributions/). NSSA eligibility must remain consistent across the effective terms for this run. It stores USD-equivalent summary amounts plus native-currency PAYE, AIDS levy, NSSA, deductions and net pay in the line snapshot. The payslip reports both native net amounts and the USD-equivalent tax basis.

Mixed runs use approved pay-date FX for a base-currency accounting journal, with the rate snapshot retained on the posting. Settlement clears the recorded net-pay liability using a company-currency bank account and/or matching foreign-currency bank accounts; it records the actual payment and does not initiate a bank transfer. Ensure the relevant foreign bank accounts and accounting mappings are configured. If NSSA POBS coverage is confirmed, the mixed calculation uses the approved USD rule ceiling against combined converted cash wages and allocates contributions by currency. The age check still uses confidential HR date-of-birth data. Other coverage exclusions remain a payroll administrator's responsibility. See [ZIMRA's mixed salary PAYE guidance](https://www.zimra.co.zw/domestic-taxes/individual/pay-as-you-earn-paye) and verify configured rates and statutory eligibility before approval.

For PAYE, BRetail classifies a midyear starter or leaver, and an employee with an HR-recorded Non-FDS reason, as Non-FDS. Non-FDS uses the current monthly table without credits. Other employees use the owner-approved FDS averaging method: taxable pay and claimed credits accumulate through the tax year, the average monthly taxable pay is taxed using the monthly table, then prior PAYE and levy are deducted. The method is snapshotted on each approved run and cannot switch midyear. Add complete, approved monthly payroll history before running FDS; if prior runs predate this method snapshot or months are missing, BRetail blocks the calculation so payroll can reconcile history. A year-to-date recalculation may produce a negative PAYE or levy amount; payroll posts the net refund due as a debit to the PAYE payable account and increases net wages accordingly. Mixed-currency payroll converts taxable pay and credits using the approved pay-date rate before applying this calculation, then allocates the result by each currency's converted taxable-income share, per the owner-approved policy.

The **TaRMS monthly PAYE workpaper** export provides branch/currency totals and employee detail from approved, non-reversed runs. It is a reconciliation file for manual review and portal entry, not an electronic filing. ZIMRA introduced a monthly PAYE return from January 2024 and said annual ITF16 is no longer relevant from the 2024 tax year; its Employee Management Module registers employees and earnings and fills PAYE/AIDs Levy after earnings are entered. Use the restricted HR profile to maintain available TIN, national ID, DOB, marital-status and disability information. BRetail does not integrate with TaRMS or generate its live portal template, so reconcile the export against the portal and use the latest TaRMS template for entry. ZIMRA requires separate FDS and Non-FDS earnings templates. Confirm each worker's classification, averaging method and calculated amount in TaRMS before filing; direct filing and generation of the live portal template remain manual. See [ZIMRA Notice 12 of 2024](https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=3706%3Apublic-notice-12-of-2024-on-submission-of-new-paye-return), [Notice 05 of 2025](https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4216%3Apublic-notice-05-of-2025-submission-of-new-paye-return), the [Non-FDS guidance](https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2343-non-fds-calculation-method), and the [Employee Management Module FAQ](https://www.zimra.co.zw/downloads/category/9-domestic-taxes?download=4221:paye-return-employee-management-module-faqs).

The HR page can export a **NSSA P4 contribution workpaper** for approved, non-reversed runs. It includes POBS employee/employer amounts and APWCS employer premiums, industry code/rate, earnings and current-period total due, including the native-currency split for mixed payroll. This is a reconciliation workpaper, not a portal upload or full assessment: reconcile with NSSA's live P4/P4A. Arrears, late registration, amounts already paid, penalties and surcharges remain separate and are excluded from payroll calculations. NSSA's record-keeping guidance lists employee identifiers and monthly contribution details, and portal filing remains manual. See [NSSA POBS coverage](https://www.nssa.org.zw/coverage/), [contribution rules and forms](https://www.nssa.org.zw/Contributions/), and [record-keeping requirements](https://www.nssa.org.zw/record-keeping/).
