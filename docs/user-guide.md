# User guide

## First setup

Open Register. Enter your name, email and a password of at least 12 characters with letters and numbers. Continue to your business details, choose a general retail, baby clothing or computer template, and select a base currency. Add accepted currencies with comma-separated three-letter codes. Enter your financial-year start and your own tax configuration; the system supplies no statutory rates.

Name the first branch and warehouse, choose an invoice prefix and receipt message, then create your workspace. Optional module selections save a future roadmap; they do not enable retail operations yet. Add a logo later in Company settings.

## Daily foundation tasks

- Overview shows your accessible branches, warehouses, core modules and company currency. Sales figures will be introduced with transaction modules.
- Branches & warehouses lets authorized managers search branches and edit their names, addresses and active status. Owners can add branches with a default warehouse. Branch codes are unique within a company.
- Team & access lets owners add a staff member with an initial password, predefined role and branch assignments. Manage access to change roles/assignments or deactivate a colleague. Share initial credentials privately. You cannot change your own access here.
- Modules shows required foundation modules and future delivery phases. Select preferences to save your roadmap.
- Company settings lets owners update identity, document preferences and a private PNG/JPEG/WebP logo. Uploaded logos must be at most 2 MB and 4096 pixels per side. Base currency and tax transaction rules are deferred to later financial phases.
- Activity log shows the latest 100 company audit events to authorized users.

On mobile, use the top-left menu. The moon/sun button saves a colour-theme preference on this device. Sign out from the profile icon at the bottom of navigation.

## Help

Validation messages explain rejected details. A forbidden page means your role, branch assignment or module status does not allow access; contact your owner. Offline sales, fiscal receipts, payment processing and financial reports remain unavailable. Core inventory is now available through the optional Inventory module.

Print this guide from your Markdown viewer for staff training.

## Inventory now available

Phase 2 core inventory is available as an optional module. Enable it in Modules, then follow [the inventory guide](inventory-guide.md). The earlier Phase 1 description above records the original foundation scope.


## Training point of sale now available

Phase 3 provides a local training POS workflow. Enable Inventory and Point of sale, then follow the [POS guide](pos-guide.md). Live checkout remains locked until Phase 4 accounting is implemented and validated. Earlier release descriptions above record their original scope.



## Phase 4 accounting update

The Phase 3 training-only boundary above describes the earlier release. Phase 4 adds base-currency accounting, journals, periods, cashbook, supplier invoice posting, bank reconciliation and financial reports. Live POS requires explicit company activation after verified setup; existing training sales are never converted. Read [accounting-guide.md](accounting-guide.md) and [accounting-api.md](accounting-api.md). Payment-provider and fiscal integrations remain outside this release.
